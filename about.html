About

I'm Vinayak Yadav, a 17-year-old aspiring developer and technology enthusiast from India. I'm currently in my second year of a Diploma in Computer Science & Engineering at Dr. B.R. Ambedkar Polytechnic College, Gwalior.

My interests span web development and programming through to creative fields like art and sketching. I enjoy working on projects that push me to learn something new — whether that's refining a user interface, working through a tricky problem in C or Python, or exploring what Linux can do. I've been learning independently through online resources, documentation, and hands-on building for the past couple of years, and I continue to do so alongside my formal studies.

Outside of code, I'm interested in open-source culture — how communities collaborate to build software, and how transparency shapes better tools. I'm early in this journey and focused on building real, functional things.
